At the baseline’s availability target: the least-cost ILP point moves left by 48.9% in the independent-price example and 25.1% in the ranked-price example. It is constrained to match or exceed the baseline’s mean and worst-RSM availability; any vertical overshoot is incidental.

Under the baseline’s budget: maximizing modeled availability reduces mean unavailability by only about 1.4% and 1.3% in these seed-0 examples—roughly 0.01 second per RSM-day. The small vertical gap is consistent with the common weekly maintenance assumption dominating placement-dependent faults.
