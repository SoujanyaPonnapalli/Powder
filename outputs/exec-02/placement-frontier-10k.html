Model boundary. The y-axis is −log₁₀(1 − mean RSM availability). Both panels share the same scales. Points below the target are valid cost-frontier points, but do not satisfy the blind baseline’s availability constraint. These are exact machine-rate scores in a simplified Markov model, which does not score regional/global outage processes or the correlation among RSMs sharing physical machines.
